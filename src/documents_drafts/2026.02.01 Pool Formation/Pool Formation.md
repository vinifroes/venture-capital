| **2. Autorização e Total de Participações (60M Common)** | **2. Authorization and Total Equity (60M Common)** |
| 2.1 Para fins de cap table, as Partes aprovam que o Capital Totalmente Diluído da Empresa será considerado como **60.000.000 (sessenta milhões) de unidades de Common** (“**Total Authorized Common**”), salvo ajuste escrito posterior aprovado pela Empresa e Founders.<br><br>2.2 Os Pools abaixo serão criados e reservados **antes** da efetivação do Equity Purchase Agreement (compra secundária de 5%), de modo que tais reservas diluam exclusivamente os Founders Originários. | 2.1 For cap table purposes, the Parties approve that the Company’s Fully Diluted Capitalization shall be deemed **60,000,000 (sixty million) units of Common** (“**Total Authorized Common**”), unless otherwise agreed in writing and approved by the Company and the Founders.<br><br>2.2 The Pools below shall be created and reserved **prior** to the effectiveness of the Equity Purchase Agreement (5% secondary purchase), so that such reserves dilute only the Original Founders. |
| **3. Criação e Reserva de Pools (Common)** | **3. Pool Creation and Reservation (Common)** |
| 3.1 As Partes aprovam a criação e reserva dos seguintes pools, todos baseados em Common (opções/awards sobre Common):<br>**(a) Advisor Pool: 3%** do Total Authorized Common = **1.800.000** Common reservadas.<br>**(b) Talent Pool: 5%** do Total Authorized Common = **3.000.000** Common reservadas.<br>**(c) Founder Performance Pool: 10%** do Total Authorized Common = **6.000.000** Common reservadas **exclusivamente** para o Beneficiário (Performance Equity Agreement).<br><br>3.2 Os Pools são reservas para fins de cap table (não constituem emissão imediata) e deverão ser operacionalizados via Plano de Incentivo/Instrumentos de Outorga aprovados pela Empresa, observadas as leis aplicáveis e o Acordo de Sócios (quando aplicável). | 3.1 The Parties approve the creation and reservation of the following pools, all based on Common (options/awards over Common):<br>**(a) Advisor Pool: 3%** of Total Authorized Common = **1,800,000** Common reserved.<br>**(b) Talent Pool: 5%** of Total Authorized Common = **3,000,000** Common reserved.<br>**(c) Founder Performance Pool: 10%** of Total Authorized Common = **6,000,000** Common reserved **exclusively** for the Beneficiary (Performance Equity Agreement).<br><br>3.2 The Pools are cap table reservations (not immediate issuances) and shall be implemented through an Incentive Plan/Grant Instruments approved by the Company, subject to applicable law and the Shareholders’ Agreement (when applicable). |
| **4. Regra de Reversão de Saldos Não Distribuídos (Pro-rata Founders)** | **4. Reversion of Undistributed Balances (Pro Rata Founders)** |
| 4.1 Qualquer parcela reservada nos Pools que **não tenha sido efetivamente outorgada/emitida** (o “**Saldo Não Distribuído**”) poderá ser cancelada/revertida mediante aprovação formal da Empresa, retornando **pro-rata** aos Founders Originários conforme a **Proporção de Reversão** fixada na Data de Referência de Reversão.<br><br>4.2 A reversão aplica-se apenas ao Saldo Não Distribuído e não afeta participações já concedidas/emitidas a terceiros.<br><br>4.3 **Exceção operacional (Founder Performance Pool):** o Saldo Não Distribuído do Founder Performance Pool somente poderá ser revertido após (i) extinção/encerramento definitivo dos direitos do Beneficiário no Performance Equity Agreement, ou (ii) declaração formal de não atingimento/renúncia ao milestone em caráter definitivo, a fim de preservar a finalidade exclusiva do pool. | 4.1 Any portion reserved in the Pools that has **not been effectively granted/issued** (the “**Undistributed Balance**”) may be cancelled/reverted upon formal Company approval and returned **pro rata** to the Original Founders in accordance with the **Reversion Ratio** fixed on the Reversion Reference Date.<br><br>4.2 Reversion applies only to the Undistributed Balance and shall not affect equity already granted/issued to third parties.<br><br>4.3 **Operational exception (Founder Performance Pool):** Undistributed Balance of the Founder Performance Pool may only be reverted after (i) definitive termination of the Beneficiary’s rights under the Performance Equity Agreement, or (ii) a formal determination that the milestone will not be achieved / has been definitively waived, to preserve the pool’s exclusive purpose. |
| **5. Integração com Equity Purchase Agreement (Compra 5% Pro-rata)** | **5. Integration with Equity Purchase Agreement (5% Pro-Rata Purchase)** |
| 5.1 As Partes reconhecem que, após a criação dos Pools, o Comprador (Beneficiário) adquirirá **5% do Total Authorized Common**, equivalente a **3.000.000** Common, por compra secundária **pro-rata** dos Founders (E e R), conforme suas participações na data do Closing do Equity Purchase Agreement.<br><br>5.2 Considerando a proporção Founder **75/25** entre E e R na Data de Referência de Reversão, a venda pro-rata de 3.000.000 Common deverá resultar, como regra, em: **E vende 2.250.000** e **R vende 750.000**, salvo ajuste escrito específico no Equity Purchase Agreement. | 5.1 The Parties acknowledge that after the Pools are created, the Buyer (Beneficiary) shall acquire **5% of Total Authorized Common**, equal to **3,000,000** Common, through a **pro-rata** secondary purchase from the Founders (E and R), according to their ownership at the Closing of the Equity Purchase Agreement.<br><br>5.2 Assuming a Founder **75/25** ratio between E and R as of the Reversion Reference Date, the pro-rata sale of 3,000,000 Common shall generally result in: **E sells 2,250,000** and **R sells 750,000**, unless otherwise expressly agreed in the Equity Purchase Agreement. |
| **6. Integração com Performance Equity Agreement (10% Exclusivo do Beneficiário)** | **6. Integration with Performance Equity Agreement (10% Exclusive to Beneficiary)** |
| 6.1 O Founder Performance Pool (10%) é criado **exclusivamente** para suportar a entrega do Performance Equity ao Beneficiário, nos termos do Performance Equity Agreement, sem utilização para quaisquer terceiros.<br><br>6.2 Caso, por exigência de investidores em rodadas futuras, seja necessário evitar diluição de investidores por recomposição de pools, as Partes reconhecem que tais ajustes deverão ser financiados exclusivamente pelos Founders (por transferência/surrender de Common), conforme previsto nos instrumentos aplicáveis. | 6.1 The Founder Performance Pool (10%) is created **exclusively** to support delivery of the Beneficiary’s Performance Equity under the Performance Equity Agreement, and may not be used for any third parties.<br><br>6.2 If, due to investor requirements in future rounds, it becomes necessary to avoid investor dilution from pool refresh, the Parties acknowledge such adjustments shall be funded exclusively by the Founders (through transfer/surrender of Common), as provided in the applicable instruments. |
